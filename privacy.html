Uni Read Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Uni Read does not collect, store, or share any personal information or data of any kind.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, ages, or contact details.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising, from us or any third party.
  • No camera, microphone, location, or contacts access.
  • No links out of the app, purchases, or chat.
  • No internet connection is needed to play. The app does not send anything over the network.

What stays on the device

The app saves the selected word list and game progress (gifts unlocked and the unicorn's outfit) on the device itself. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children's privacy

Uni Read is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws without needing parental consent for data collection.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
